02

Reflection makes the lesson legible.

People assess usefulness. The AI tool examines its contribution, checks and uncertainty. Reflection captures lessons for a later decision.

Retrospectives

Reflect before a lesson becomes knowledge.

People

The requester and people doing the work answer in their own words.

  • What went well?
  • What did not go well?
  • What was learned?
  • What still puzzles us?

AI tool

The tool examines its own contribution against the retained work.

  • Which drafts or checks helped?
  • Where did its output need correction?
  • Which tests or evidence support the result?
  • What remains uncertain?

Capture the accounts and their evidence together. Then decide which observations remain local, need correction or merit a proposed change.

Why require a requester retrospective?

Reflection captures learning for later use. This work is a foundry, not a factory: I pause mid-build when a lesson needs attention.

The requester completes a retrospective before release; their experience can expose needs the checks missed.

Evidence and checks

Keep the source, tests, limitations and exact version that was reviewed.

Requester decision

Record acceptance or rejection and the reason against that version. Release is a separate decision.
