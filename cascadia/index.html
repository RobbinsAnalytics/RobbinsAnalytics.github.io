02

Five capabilities supporting the work.

Each capability has a distinct responsibility. Expand a definition for its working contents, then follow their interaction below.

Sovereign Ground

Keep ownership, authoritative sources and continuity clear.

Which version is authoritative, and where does it belong?

What is included in Sovereign Ground?
Authoritative homes
Source ownership, catalogs, provenance and the location of maintained assets.
Knowledge continuity
Decisions, source registers, session records and references that preserve context.
Portability and custody
Version identity, retained artifacts, access boundaries and reliable handoffs.

These are responsibility areas. A final module taxonomy and unified lifecycle registry remain in development.

Foundation Readiness

Establish a usable foundation for a declared application.

What exists, what is usable, and what needs attention first?

What is included in Foundation Readiness?
Inventory
Identify existing data, documents, tools, definitions, constraints and owners.
Rationalize
Resolve duplication, conflicting sources, gaps and responsibilities within the agreed scope.
Operationalize
Make the selected arrangement usable and verify access, configuration and behavior.

Inventory, Rationalize and Operationalize are process stages, rather than three separate modules.

Solutions Foundry

Turn a defined need into a useful solution, with evidence and retained learning.

What needs to be delivered, and what will count as success?

What is included in Solutions Foundry?
Brief and scope
Define the decision, audience, inputs, intended output and acceptance conditions.
Build and correct
Use an appropriate delivery profile, from a small incremental micro-build to an analytical module.
Compare and learn
Preserve versions, compare when commissioned, and capture human feedback and technical observations.
Deliver and retain
Keep the source, evidence, acceptance decision and next action linked to the delivered version.

Maintained brief, module-build, micro-build, comparison and retrospective procedures exist. Wider intake and orchestration continue to evolve.

Experience Framework

Shape how people encounter, understand and use the work.

Can the intended audience understand it and act?

What is included in Experience Framework?
Web Design
Page structure, responsive layout, navigation, interactions and reusable components.
Visualization
Quantitative meaning, chart expression, provenance, accessibility and reader interpretation.
Documents
Document, email and export presentation suited to their use.
Identity
Marks, typography and coherent identity application across outputs.

These are the four documented modules. A company or project supplies its applicable profile and constraints.

Trust Assurance

Establish the evidence supporting confidence and acceptance.

What has been checked, what remains uncertain, and who accepts the result?

What is included in Trust Assurance?
Data Integrity
Source identity, completeness, conformance and retained data boundaries.
Analytical Validity
Business definitions, expected cases, calculations and appropriately separate derivation.
Experience Assurance
Readability, accessibility, interaction paths and intended understanding.
Engineering Controls
Source, build and change controls relevant to the application.
Operational Reliability
Run behavior, reconciliation, recovery and visible failures.
Evidence and Acceptance
Version-linked findings, limits and an accountable human decision.

This is the six-module working scope. Existing checks support parts of it; the umbrella is not a claim of a unified assurance service.
