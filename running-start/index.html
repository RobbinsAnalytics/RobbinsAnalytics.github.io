Proposal for Santosh

Introductions. Context. A running start.

Project
Running Start

Santosh,

You mentioned how much context it takes to get fully up to speed at SafeRide. I’d like to start building that context before November 24 by meeting the people I’ll be working with, introducing myself, and learning about their roles and how they use data and analytics.

These are the conversations I’d want to have starting on day one. Project Running Start simply brings them forward where people have time.

I’d start with the VP of AI, as we discussed, then Ben, you, Kishore, and Srikanth. From there, I’d follow introductions to other people they recommend. Each conversation would be about 30 minutes, scheduled around their availability. There’s no need to fit everyone in before my start date.

What the conversations would cover

After introductions and some context about their role, I’d explain that I’m asking with data and reporting in mind:

  1. What are your biggest challenges right now, and where does data make them harder?

  2. Where could better data or reporting make the biggest difference?

  3. What would make my role a success for you at 90 days?

  4. One or two questions tailored to the person—for example: How does your team currently use data and analytics in its day-to-day work, and what works well?

  5. If you were me, what would you focus on?

  6. Who else should I be talking to?

The goal is to get to know people, understand their work, and learn where I can be useful. These conversations can stay at the level of roles, workflows, and experiences; they don’t require system access, company datasets, or patient information.

I’d send each person a short, private recap so they can correct anything I misunderstood. Where useful, I’d follow up with a small illustrative example using synthetic data to make my understanding easier to check. That’s part of how I learn, rather than a commitment to start delivering operational work before joining.

What I’d need from you

  • Your support to begin these conversations before my start date, including any boundaries on people or topics.

  • The VP of AI introduction we discussed, and help getting the first conversations arranged.

  • Confirmation of who should handle invitations inside SafeRide. I’ll keep my availability current; the person making each introduction or an internal coordinator can send the meeting invite.

  • Your support for transcribing the calls, with each person’s agreement, and guidance on the approved way to store and process the transcripts and share individual recaps.

The result I’m aiming for is straightforward: arrive knowing more of the team, understanding their work, and better prepared to learn during onboarding.
