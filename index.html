AI Analytics Architect

Trust What You Can Test

12+ years turning messy data into decisions leaders trust: healthcare manufacturing and quality, ecommerce and retail, and time in supply chain, digital merchandising, and finance. The expertise is in the craft, not the tooling. I design the analytics, specify what correct looks like, and let AI do the build. Every build carries its own evidence: known answers set before the code, tests that run every time, and a record of what each test caught. That is proof stakeholders can check, not a claim they have to trust.

Open to analytics manager, lead, or principal roles where reporting has outgrown trust, and to conversations about roles that don’t exist yet. Seattle or remote.

Mathematics · A pet project

The Combinatorics of Sibling Conflict

Not an analytics module: a short mathematics paper. The number of ways n children can take sides against each other, with the rest uninvolved, is (3n − 2·2n + 1)/2, the Stirling number S(n+1, 3). Of the 25 ways four children can, 6 are one against one; of the 3,025 ways eight children can, 28 are.
