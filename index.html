Method

Specify, validate, operate

Explore the Cascadia operating system ↗

  1. 01Specify

    Validation Playbook

    Written spec

    Specification → agent build → review

    I set the architecture, the data model and the measurement definitions as a written specification. AI coding agents build to it. I do not hand-write the Python: I write the spec, review the output and validate it.

  2. 02Validate

    Revenue Assurance

    Independent check

    Definition → independent SQL → reconcile

    A separately written calculation checks a published figure against its definition. Revenue Assurance derives every published cell twice, by two engines written to be different, and they agree. Claim Integrity also publishes its numerical agreement and the separate limits of its assurance record.

  3. 03Operate

    Matter Ledger

    Operated, not only built

    Schedule → re-assert → publish failures

    A surface that has only ever shown green has demonstrated nothing. Matter Ledger runs on a schedule, re-asserts its own invariants on every run, reconciles a live increment against the frozen baseline, and publishes the result, including when a run fails.

Mathematics · A pet project

The Combinatorics of Sibling Conflict

Not an analytics module: a short mathematics paper. The number of ways n children can take sides against each other, with the rest uninvolved, is (3n − 2·2n + 1)/2, the Stirling number S(n+1, 3). Of the 25 ways four children can, 6 are one against one; of the 3,025 ways eight children can, 28 are.
