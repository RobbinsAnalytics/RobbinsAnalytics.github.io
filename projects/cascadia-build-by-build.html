The problem

Make the evidence
easier to inspect.

Separate project pages explain individual builds. A shared inventory makes their controls comparable without turning the portfolio into a ranking.

01

Find the check

Rebuilds, source governance, numerical checks, presentation review and operation sit in a common vocabulary.

02

Read its actual state

A report can exist while a check fails, remains deferred or covers only part of the requirement. Its presence alone cannot establish a pass.

03

The original ten builds retain every recorded cell. Older projects are not retrofitted to fill the table, and an absent historical artifact creates no remediation debt.

○

This is a categorical inventory. Artifact counts are not quality percentages, maturity scores or business-performance measures.

The Claim Integrity extension

Expected work.
Completed checks.

The original matrix covered ten builds and fourteen surfaces. Claim Integrity adds a build and the expected-event definition, while retaining the earlier evidence.

15 / Operation

What should have arrived?

Expected event accounting starts from independently retained triggering facts. A claim never constructed or a promised response never received remains expected work, with its cutoff and timing basis.

The obligation itself is explicit, extending population reconciliation beyond the records that arrived.

Architecture and provenance
Original baseline
data/builds.json and data/standard.json retain their October 7 freeze at 66b9de6. No reread or retrofit of those ten repositories is implied.
Claim Integrity extension
Repository evidence is pinned to 9e21fc7. Recorded outcomes come from the frozen public verification snapshot. Served snapshot bytes and later repository artifact bytes retain separate identities where they differ.
Deterministic inventory
src/extend_inventory.py combines the baseline and authored extension. Check mode verifies the unchanged baseline, extension hashes, row and column shape, and explicit not-assessed states.
Publication builder
src/build_expanded_page.py renders the matrix, definitions, scope notes and historical design-standard lane. Website counts and its miniature use the same expanded inventory.

The extension is additive. It does not claim all eleven repositories were audited on one date.

Interpretation

What the checks establish

The retained inventory can be reproduced, its evidence can be followed and its current interface can be reviewed.

An earlier panel did not review this redesigned layout. No new blind panel or assurance cycle is claimed for this UI update.

A recorded artifact can carry failed, blocked, deferred, open or historical results. Those states remain visible.
